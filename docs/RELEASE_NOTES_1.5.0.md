## DiskFrame 1.5.0

### All Drives
- **Browse several drives as one library.** On the drive page, open **All Drives** to see the photos and videos already indexed on every drive (or the ones you choose with **Choose drives…**) together, newest first. Search, filters, favourites and trash work across them. Nothing is copied or moved, and each drive can still be opened on its own exactly as before.
- **Your choice is remembered**, by each drive's identity rather than its letter, so it survives restarts and letter changes. A chip in the gallery header shows which drives are included and opens the same chooser.
- **Disconnected drives stay listed.** Their files keep the thumbnails they already had and are marked as not connected. They are never opened, and DiskFrame never shows another drive's file in their place, even when that drive now has their old letter.

### Places and Trips, found on this PC
- **Events now finds places and trips by itself**, from the location and capture time each photo or video recorded. Places are spots you returned to; trips are stays of two days or more away from where you usually were at the time.
- **Everything happens on this computer.** Places are not looked up online, and no location, file name or picture is sent anywhere. A place is named by you.
- **Suggestions are only suggestions:** keep or dismiss them, rename, merge two places, move photos into a new one, choose the cover, and add or remove photos.
- **Make your own** Places, Trips and collections, with or without GPS: right-click any photo or video and choose **Add to collection…**. You can give a collection a place and dates.
- **Optional home:** open a place and choose **This is my home**. DiskFrame never guesses it.
- Places and trips are found per drive. You can choose to combine drives in Events → settings.

### Events, all in one place
- One page with **All, Calendar, Places, Trips, Collections and Suggested**.
- **Google Calendar** stays optional. When it is connected, you choose which drives' photos are matched to your calendar (Settings → Google Calendar). A newly connected drive starts off, so photos on someone else's drive are never sorted into your events.

### Also
- The installed app no longer ships readable source code, and a list of third-party licences is included.

### Still the same
- Connecting a Google account is **not switched on yet** in this release; Settings and Events say so clearly. Places, Trips and collections work without it.

### Updating
- **From 1.1.0 or later:** DiskFrame offers this update by itself. Your library, thumbnails, favourites and settings are kept; the update only adds new tables and settings.
- **From 1.0.0 or 1.0.1:** download `diskframe-1.5.0-setup.exe` below and run it once; your library and settings are kept, and later versions arrive automatically.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: choose **More info → Run anyway**. The in-app update verifies the download's checksum either way.
