## DiskFrame 1.4.0

### Events (new)
- A new **Events** section in the dock. Connect your Google Calendar in **Settings → Google Calendar** and DiskFrame shows your events as large cover cards, by year, each with the photos and videos you took during it.
- Open an event to see its files. DiskFrame suggests the files taken during the event; you can **confirm** or **reject** each suggestion, **add** any other file by hand, **remove** one, and **undo** the last change.
- Read-only access: DiskFrame only reads events from calendars you own and never changes your calendar. Your sign-in is stored encrypted on this PC with Windows' own protection. File names, paths, photos and thumbnails never leave your PC; matching happens on your PC. **Disconnect** in Settings removes the sign-in and stops syncing.
- **Photos** stays the date gallery you know.

### Dates you can trust
- Days, months and years are grouped by **your local date**. A photo taken at 02:00 no longer lands on the previous day.
- DiskFrame records where every date came from (camera time with time zone, camera time, video recording time, or the file's own dates) and shows it in **Info**. Camera time zones are honoured. Your existing library is updated in the background; nothing on your drives is changed.

### Stability
- **A bad sector on an external drive no longer closes DiskFrame.** Reading a damaged file while making a thumbnail could end the whole app; now that one file shows no preview and everything else carries on.

### Updating
- **From 1.1.0 or later:** DiskFrame offers this update by itself. Your library, thumbnails, favourites and settings are kept.
- **From 1.0.0 or 1.0.1:** download `diskframe-1.4.0-setup.exe` below and run it once; your library and settings are kept, and later versions arrive automatically.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: choose **More info → Run anyway**. The in-app update verifies the download's checksum either way.
