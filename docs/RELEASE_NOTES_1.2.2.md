## DiskFrame 1.2.2

### Drag and drop
- **Dragging files out of DiskFrame** (to Explorer, the desktop or another app) now shows the file itself: its thumbnail on a rounded card, a small play mark for videos, and a stack with a count when you drag several. The red play icon is gone.
- **Drop files or folders from Explorer onto DiskFrame** to add them to your library. A quiet frame shows where they will land; when you let go, DiskFrame says how many were added, how many were already there and which were not supported. Nothing is ever added twice.
- Files dropped from another drive are added to that drive's library, and the message says so.

Windows draws the drag image slightly see-through and adds its own "Copy to…" label over the place you drop on; that part belongs to Windows.

### Updating
- **From 1.1.0 or later:** DiskFrame offers this update by itself.
- **From 1.0.0 or 1.0.1:** download `diskframe-1.2.2-setup.exe` below and run it once; your library and settings are kept.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the first time.
